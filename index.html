Controls

  • Select: left click or drag a box. Shift adds to the selection. Double click selects all of that type on screen. Ctrl+A selects everyone on screen.
  • Right click is the smart command: ground moves, an enemy attacks, your camp, mine, tapper or refinery puts them to work (a Truck hauls 120 a trip), your tower, Bunker or HQ garrisons it, your Truck is boarded, your unfinished line gets dug on. With a camp, mine, tapper, refinery or Depot selected, right click one of your Depots to make it the supply link.
  • Unit commands: F attack-move (click an enemy or a point; mortars bombard the point), R defend position, G retreat towards base, X stop, E enter (click a camp, mine, tapper, refinery, Scout Tower, Bunker, the HQ or a Truck), Q unload selected Trucks, K fill a trench (Workers), V throw a grenade (Riflemen, after the research). After their research: M mortars fire smoke, L mortars fire a flare, C a soldier blows up a barricade, wire or bridge. Hold Shift to queue orders back to back.
  • Suppressed units (yellow ring) ignore target orders and fire at the closest enemy. Snipers keep their orders, take half the stress and never panic. Retreating units shed stress twice as fast and are not slowed by it.
  • B opens the Build tab; while it is open a letter picks what to build: L Lumber Camp, M Mine, Z Rubber Tapper, F Refinery, C Barracks, O Ordnance Works, K Workshop, G Depot, R R&D Lab, T Scout Tower, U Bunker, P Field Hospital. Lines: Y trench, I barricade, J barbed wire, E road, V bridge: hold the left button and drag; the selected soldiers dig defences (Workers build roads and bridges), or idle Workers within 300 m if none are selected. N research overview. O army overview (every unit by type and what it is doing; click a number to select those units). I selects the next idle Worker. J jumps to the latest "under attack" alert. H jump to headquarters. Tab cycles factories.
  • With a factory selected: Z X C V train the listed units (Tab shows the next four if there are more), right click a unit in its list to train it on repeat (again to stop), right click the map sets the rally point. With a tower selected: T upgrade. With a tower, Bunker or HQ selected: Q unload everyone. With a Truck selected, its panel shows fuel, passengers and Retrofit (next to a Workshop).
  • Squadrons: Ctrl+1..9 with 2 to 12 soldiers selected forms squadron 1..9 (a soldier is in one squadron at a time); with nothing selected it clears it. 1..9 or a click on the squadron bar selects, press twice to centre the view. Orders to any member move the whole squadron in formation; right-drag sets the width and facing of its line. Right click a squad member with a factory selected to send new units to that squadron.
  • Camera: W A S D, arrow keys, screen edge, or hold the middle mouse button and drag. Mouse wheel zooms. Click or right click the minimap.
  • Time: Space pause, , slower, . faster. 1× is a calm pace; 2× is the original tempo.
  • Weather and night: the line next to the clock shows the weather, the time of day and a forecast a minute ahead. At night everyone sees half as far, a soldier who fires is seen for 3 s, and mortars fire only at what your side can see (flares and searchlights help). Rain slows movement off-road and spoils aim, fog cuts vision, snow slows infantry and digging. Direct fire needs your side to see the target.
  • High ground: more vision, more range and damage firing downhill. Ridges block sight and bullets. Steep slopes crawl or are impassable. Forests hide units and give cover. A Scout Tower adds height to whoever is inside.
  • Esc cancels a command or clears the selection.

Press F1 or click anywhere to close.
